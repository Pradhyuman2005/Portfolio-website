Overview

Ultimate Energy approached Fisher Digital for a complete website redesign. Following the visual direction established in the initial pages designed by our Senior Designer, I designed the "About Us" and "Contact Us" screens to ensure strict consistency in typography, layout, and component styling. I then fully developed and deployed both pages in WordPress.

Problem

Ultimate Energy's website suffered from complex navigation and poor SEO performance, creating high friction for prospective clients searching for their services and hindering lead generation.

To accelerate the workflow, I leveraged Figma AI for rapid high-fidelity layout generation, maintaining strict visual consistency with the pages established by our Senior Designer. Utilizing my core design expertise, I critically evaluated AI-generated components, identified structural and spacing flaws, and manually refined the layouts to ensure a polished, production-ready output.

What we achieved

  • Restructuring key touchpoints like the "About Us" and "Contact Us" pages eliminated layout clutter, making essential company information intuitive and effortless for clients to find.
  • Utilizing Figma AI for rapid prototyping allowed for faster exploration, while applying critical design oversight ensured every layout flaw was corrected to meet strict production standards.
  • Adhering closely to the Senior Designer's established visual patterns, component libraries, and typography ensured a seamless user experience across the entire website.
  • Translating the refined designs into clean WordPress builds resolved technical layout friction and established a solid foundation for better search engine visibility.

Reflection

What’s Next?

A/B Testing & SEO Monitoring: Track organic traffic growth, bounce rates, and user conversion metrics in WordPress post-launch to measure search engine performance and make data-driven layout refinements.

Expanding Reusable Component Libraries: Build out additional custom block templates inside WordPress to give the client more flexibility when creating future landing pages without breaking visual consistency.

Enhancing Micro-Interactions: Integrate subtle hover states and interactive form feedback across the "Contact Us" flow to make reaching out feel more fluid and engaging for prospective clients.

Lessons Learned

  • Using Figma AI accelerated early iterations, but relying solely on AI outputs can introduce layout and spacing flaws. Active design oversight was essential to refine AI concepts into cohesive, high-quality screens.
  • Adhering to the Senior Designer’s established visual language taught me the value of thinking about systems and working within pre-existing constraints, ensuring the final "About Us" and "Contact Us" pages felt completely integrated into the broader website.
  • Building the final designs in WordPress reinforced the need to design pragmatically, ensuring custom high-fidelity layouts translate smoothly into functional, performance-ready web pages without introducing technical friction.
